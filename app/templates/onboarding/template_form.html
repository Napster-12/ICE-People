How it's laid out

  • A blank line starts a new paragraph; lines inside a paragraph stay on their own line (an address).
  • "# RE: …" is the bold, underlined subject line.
  • A short line with text under it (no full stop), like "1. Remuneration", becomes a bold heading.
  • Lines like "Start date: {{ '{{ start_date }}' }}" become a two-column list.
  • Lines starting with "- " become bullets, and **text** is bold.
  • [signature] is the signature image; [page break] starts a new page.
